Automation System Guide

What This System Does

Your Lightspeed register and GoHighLevel CRM now talk to each other automatically. No more double-entry, no more forgetting to update a client profile, no more manually tracking deposits.

Deposits
Client pays deposit online → store credit appears at the register automatically, appointment confirmed.
Sales Sync
Every sale at the register updates the client's GHL profile: tags, artist, dollar amounts, and a sale note.
No-Shows & Cancellations
Mark a no-show or late cancel in GHL and the store credit is reversed automatically. Client is tagged.
Nightly Sweep
Every night at 11:30 PM, a reconciliation catches anything that was missed during the day.
Removed from scope: Waiver archiving (GHL handles it natively) and low-stock SMS alerts (Lightspeed handles it natively) were originally planned but are no longer needed.

Step-by-Step: Manager & Artist Guide

Here's what you need to know for each situation you'll encounter day to day.

BOOKING A new client wants to book a tattoo appointment
  1. Open GHL Calendar and create the appointment for the client.
  2. Set the status to "Unconfirmed" — this is what triggers the deposit link to be sent.
  3. The system automatically sends the client a $100 deposit payment link.
  4. When the client pays, their appointment flips to "Confirmed" automatically.
  5. At the register: their $100 store credit is already there — apply it at checkout.
Important: The default calendar status is "Confirmed" which does NOT send the deposit link. You must manually set it to "Unconfirmed" to trigger the payment link.

Note: Currently only $100 deposits are available. Additional tiers ($50, $100, $250) are coming soon.
CHECKOUT A client arrives for their appointment
  1. Check them in on the GHL calendar.
  2. Do the tattoo or piercing.
  3. Ring them up at the Lightspeed register.
  4. Make sure their email address is on the sale. This is how the two systems match customers.
  5. Complete the sale as normal.
Automatic: The system updates their GHL profile with service tags (tattoo, piercing), artist name, sale amount, lifetime value, and a full sale note. If they've spent $500+ lifetime, they're tagged VIP.
NO-SHOW A client doesn't show up
  1. In GHL Calendar, mark the appointment as "No Show".
  2. That's it.
Automatic: The system reverses their store credit at the register, tags them as [client] tattoo: no-show (or piercing), and adds a note to their GHL profile.
LATE CANCEL Client cancels less than 48 hours before
  1. Cancel the appointment in GHL Calendar.
  2. That's it.
Automatic: The store credit is forfeited (no refund). The client is tagged [client] tattoo: late-cancel and a note is added to their profile.
EARLY CANCEL Client cancels 48+ hours before
  1. Cancel the appointment in GHL Calendar.
  2. The system automatically reverses the store credit.
  3. You'll receive an email: "Process Stripe refund for [client name]"
  4. Go into Stripe and issue the refund manually.
FORMS Looking up a client's release form
  1. Open the client's contact in GHL.
  2. Navigate to the Release Form page (link in GHL navigation).
  3. The form loads automatically showing their signed waiver, contact info, and any uploaded files.
AUTO Things that happen automatically (no action needed)
  • Every night at 11:30 PM, a reconciliation sweep catches any sales that didn't sync during the day.
  • Client profiles in GHL are tagged automatically with: service type, artist name, VIP status ($500+ lifetime), piercing location, and jewelry style.
  • If any workflow fails, an error email is sent to the ops team with full details.

Key Things to Remember

  • Always enter the client's email address at checkout — no email means no sync
  • When booking manually, set status to "Unconfirmed" to trigger the deposit link
  • Don't manually edit GHL tags that start with [client] or [system] — the automation manages those
  • If something seems wrong, check the client's email first — mismatched emails are the #1 cause of sync issues
